How to read this

Cardstock is the most in the 4x3/smush lineage: friendly, tactile, casual-first. Pennant has the strongest baseball soul and ages beautifully, but its density suits sickos more than casuals. Scoreboard owns the finale and the sim ticker like nothing else, at the cost of a heavier feel spin-to-spin.

They compose: one direction can carry the interface while another owns a single moment (Cardstock game + Scoreboard season-sim is a natural pairing). Team identity in all three comes from color washes per franchise — no logos needed.
